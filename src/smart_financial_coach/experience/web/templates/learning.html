The loop, step by step
  1. Corrections fix your view at once. A correction applies to that person straight away, with no retraining, and stays theirs.
  2. Agreement turns corrections into labels. A merchant's category becomes a training label only when at least {{ replay.config.rule.n }} different people hold a view on it, two thirds agree, and at least one of them chose the category rather than accepting a suggestion.
  3. Retraining. Every {{ replay.config.every }} months, with new labels, a candidate model is trained on them, plus the original training data.
  4. Gates. The candidate must do at least as well as the current model for people who supplied no labels, on data it never saw, without hurting merchants it already knew or its confidence.
  5. Promoted or rejected. A model that passes replaces the current one; one that fails is set aside.
