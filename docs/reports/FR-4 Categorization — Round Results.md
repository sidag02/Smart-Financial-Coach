# FR-4 Categorization — Round Results

Oct 3, 2026 · @Sidd · Milestones 3 and 4 of FR-4 Unseen Merchant Categorization — Feature Design (test results and promotion added after milestone 3)

## Summary

- **The round ran as the design fixed it:** three baselines and four candidates (bge-base and bge-small, balanced or no class weights), each with a clean-label shipping twin, on the regenerated dataset (40% holdout, data hash `44781bc4e4a5`). Ranking used validation data only; the test set was scored once afterwards, by `finalize` (milestone 4).
- **Rank 1: `21_small_unweighted`** (bge-small, no class weights). Its twin scores **0.712** (0.63–0.78) on validation unseen merchants, with known merchants at 0.982. It reached rank 1 through the tie-break the owner adopted before the round: tied with bge-base on F1 (comparison runs) and on unseen Brier (twins, 0.140 against 0.137), it wins on batch cost, 1.34 s against 3.73 s per 10k transactions. The design said in advance that this rule would likely favour bge-small.
- **Promoted (milestone 4): `21_small_unweighted.ship`, version `20eea4fb-44781bc4-c0274576`.** On the test set, scored once, it passes every gate: known 0.983 (≥ 0.90, keyword 0.470) and unseen **0.735** (0.67–0.81; ≥ 0.66, keyword 0.462). [Test results](#test-results-and-promotion-oct-3-2026).
- **No reversal:** no eligible twin beats rank 1's twin on validation. bge-base's twin scores 0.719, inside the paired interval.
- **No class weights wins again,** as in the POC: twins at 0.712–0.719 against 0.671–0.686 with balanced weights.
- **The v1 gate was lowered from 0.70 to 0.66 (owner, Oct 3, 2026), on these validation results and before any test scoring.** Rank 1's twin sits 0.012 above 0.70: with the re-measured spread it would pass 0.70 on a fresh 115-merchant test set only about **63%** of the time (about **53%** allowing for its being the best of four). The 0.70 target was accepted when the POC's leader stood at 0.745, with a pass rate of "roughly eight to nine draws in ten"; 0.66 restores that rate for today's leader. It is recorded in the FR-4 design (§4) as a rule change made after seeing validation results. [Details](#the-owners-decision-before-milestone-4).
- **Health & Fitness is the weakest category** (F1 0.105). The model rarely predicts it (554 rows predicted against 3,372 true), sending its rows to Utilities (32%) and Shopping (27%). Entertainment (0.492) loses rows to Dining and draws Dining's in turn.

## Setup

- **Data:** the default dataset after FR-4's milestone 1: holdout share 0.4, seed 8, schema 4. Validation folds hold out the holdout-eligible merchants of train users, 3 folds (112 unseen merchants pooled), plus a 10% seen-merchant row sample per fold.
- **Candidates:** `categorization/calibrated` (per familiarity group, `method: auto`) around `linear_text` with C = 1.0, as in the launch round and the POC. Comparison runs train under the task's 2% injected label noise; their twins set `label_noise: 0`. Configs: `configs/experiments/categorization/fr4/`.
- **Rule:** the FR-4 decision rule (§5). Eligibility, ranking and the F1 tie set come from comparison runs. Tie-breaks come from the twins: unseen Brier with a paired merchant-bootstrap tie test against the Brier leader, then batch cost, then complexity. A twin beating rank 1's twin stops `finalize`.
- **Time:** 57.5 minutes for all eleven runs on a laptop CPU. bge-base comparison runs took 11–14 minutes and bge-small 6–7; twins took 3–6 minutes, because embeddings were already cached.

## Findings

1. **How rank 1 was reached.** On comparison runs, bge-base without weights has the best point estimate (0.583), and bge-small without weights ties it (paired interval −0.037 to +0.076). Their twins also tie on unseen Brier, so batch cost decides: bge-small does a 10k-row batch in 1.34 s against bge-base's 3.73 s. The two balanced candidates fall outside the F1 tie set.
2. **Clean labels, again the large effect:** +0.13 to +0.24 unseen F1 from comparison run to twin, and unseen Brier falls from 0.18–0.22 to about 0.14.
3. **Class weights:** without them, both embedding sizes gain about 0.03–0.04 on clean labels and much more under noise, as the POC found.
4. **bge-base and bge-small are tied** on every quality measure here (unseen F1 0.719 against 0.712, Brier 0.137 against 0.140, known 0.982 for both).
5. **The keyword baseline** scores 0.455 unseen and 0.464 known on validation. The comparison runs with balanced weights (0.445, 0.436) score below it on unseen merchants, but eligibility checks known merchants only, and their twins (0.686, 0.671) clear it easily.
6. **Where rank 1's errors go** (table above):
   - Health & Fitness is almost never predicted: 554 rows against 3,372 true. Its rows go to Utilities (32%) and Shopping (27%), and Shopping makes up 43% of what it does get.
   - Entertainment loses 29% of its rows to Dining, and 54% of what it's given is Dining.
   - Childcare & Education and Insurance & Fees are the next weakest (0.555, 0.658).

   Per the owner's decision, these are diagnosed now and fixed after launch, by feedback.

The holdout seed changed, and so did the 112 merchants the folds hold out. The pass rate uses a normal approximation; "0.01 lower" allows for rank 1 being the best of four candidates.

## The owner's decision, before milestone 4

`finalize` scores the test sets once, and a gate failure there is investigated under the rule, not resolved by promoting #2. So the gate was settled now, before any test score was seen. The options were:

1. proceed at 0.70 (about a 40–50% chance of failing on the draw);
2. **revisit the v1 target on validation evidence only;**
3. keep 0.70 and ship the cold model on FR-3's gates, reaching 0.70 through feedback.

**Decided (owner, Oct 3, 2026): option 2, a v1 gate of 0.66.**
- It applies the reasoning that set 0.70: a gate a model as good as the leader passes on roughly eight to nine draws in ten. At 0.712 with SD 0.038, that's 0.66–0.67, or 0.65–0.66 allowing for best-of-four.
- It rejects a model like the old noisy configuration (about 0.45 here). The balanced-weights twins (0.67–0.69) would sit just above it, so the gate separates clean-label models from noisy ones; the decision rule chooses among clean candidates.
- **0.80 stays the goal for v1.1**, through feedback (FR-5/FR-6).

It's a rule change made after seeing validation results, and it's labelled as such in the FR-4 design (§4) and in the code (`UNSEEN_GATE`). No test data was involved.

## Test results and promotion (Oct 3, 2026)

`sfc-experiment finalize` scored the twins of the decision rule's top three, and the three baselines, on the fresh test set (115 holdout merchants) once. The table is above. Nothing was re-ranked on these numbers.

1. **Rank 1 passes every gate and was promoted:** known-merchant macro F1 0.983 (gate 0.90; keyword 0.470) and unseen-merchant macro F1 **0.735** (0.67–0.81; gate 0.66; keyword 0.462). Version `20eea4fb-44781bc4-c0274576`, from run `6bc58706`. Its model file is in the GitHub Release `categorization-20eea4fb-44781bc4-c0274576`, tagged at the training commit `f53643f`. The promotion log records `"task_params": {"label_noise": 0}`. A clean copy of `artifacts/` without the model file downloads it from the release and passes the manifest checksum.
2. **It would also have passed the original 0.70.** The gate was lowered to 0.66 before this score existed, on validation evidence alone (§ "The owner's decision"). The draw came out above both thresholds, so the change didn't decide this promotion. It stays the v1 gate for later retraining.
3. **The test order matches validation:** rank 1 (0.735) ≥ #2 (0.725) ≥ #3 (0.708) on unseen merchants, all three within each other's intervals. Known merchants are 0.983–0.984 for all three.
4. **FR-3's known issues are gone in the promoted model:**
   - **Travel fallback:** on the whole default dataset, at test users' unseen merchants, it predicts Travel 158 times against 142 true transactions. FR-3's model made 23,191 such predictions against 176 true, on its own holdout. Accuracy at those merchants is 0.824.
   - **Under-confidence on known merchants:** known-merchant ECE is 0.009, and 95% of rows reach confidence 0.9 (FR-3's model: 0.153 and 33%).
5. **Exact-string lookup no longer beats the model on all test users:** 0.869 against 0.943. The keyword baseline no longer edges it on unseen merchants: 0.462 against 0.735.
6. **Serving cost, measured** (`sfc-model predict`, default dataset, laptop CPU, one process): 1,098,502 transactions in 41.8 s, about **26,300 rows per second** (twice bge-base's), 1.25 GB peak memory at 20k-row batches, 0.6 s to load.
